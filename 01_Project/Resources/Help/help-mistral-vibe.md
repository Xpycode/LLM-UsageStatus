# Mistral Vibe

Mistral Vibe is billed per token and has no usage service or allowance. Everything in this section is computed from Vibe's local session files, so cost figures are estimates.

## What it shows

- **Sessions**
- **Tokens (24 hours)**, **Tokens (7 days)** and **Tokens (all time)**
- **Estimated cost** — token counts multiplied by a built-in price list. It is an estimate, not a bill. Your invoice from the provider is the real figure.
- **Skipped sessions** and **Skipped malformed records** appear only when some data could not be read.

## Unpriced sessions

If a session used a model that is not in the price list, it is counted in tokens but not in cost. The cost then shows as a minimum, for example "$4.20+ · 2 sessions unpriced". If no session can be priced, the cost row is hidden.

## Monthly budget

Right-click the panel and choose **Set Vibe Monthly Budget…**. Enter a dollar amount and the day of the month the budget resets, then choose **Save**. Leave the amount empty to remove the budget.

With a budget set, the headline shows an estimated percent left ("Monthly budget") and an approximate dollars-per-day amount. The headline appears only when every session could be priced; otherwise it is hidden, because a partial sum would overstate what is left.

Without a budget, the section shows a one-line summary of the last 24 hours: estimated spend where prices resolve, plus the token count.
