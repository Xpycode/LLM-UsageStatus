# Codex

The Codex section reads your Codex sign-in and asks Codex's usage service for your current allowance. These values are authoritative.

## What it shows

- **Weekly limit** — percent left, a countdown to the weekly reset and a bar. This is the section headline.
- **Credits** — your credit balance. When a balance exists, the headline also shows an approximate per-day amount in credits.
- **Reset passes** — how many reset passes are available, or "Unavailable".
- **Next reset-pass expiry** or **Earliest reported pass expiry** — when a pass expires, if Codex reports it.
- **5-hour limit** — optional, see below.

## Show 5-hour limit

Right-click the panel and choose **Show 5-hour limit** to add the 5-hour limit as a second headline, with its percent left, countdown and bar, plus its meter and reset time in the expanded section. A checkmark means it is on. Choose it again to hide it. The setting is remembered, and the open panel updates immediately. It only affects the Codex section; Claude Code always shows its 5-hour limit.

## If the section is empty

If it says "Not detected on this Mac.", Codex is not installed or you have not signed in to it. Sign in with Codex, then reopen the panel.
